Hello, I’m Aiya

My design journey began when I started my own clothing brand and taught myself design from the ground up. With no formal background at the time, I learned through trial and error, constantly improving by listening to real customer feedback. That experience sparked my passion for UX/UI design and problem-solving through design.

I later moved to Canada to study UX/UI design at Cornerstone College, where I gained hands-on experience as a co-op designer at Morethink Solutions. There, I helped launch a product called Fanciti, working closely with the team to shape the product from early ideas to real features.

Currently, I work as a freelance designer at Motiv Creative Labs while expanding my skills in web development. My goal is to create meaningful digital products from scratch—combining thoughtful design with technical understanding to bring ideas to life.
